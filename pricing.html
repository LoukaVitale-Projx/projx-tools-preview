Methodology

This analysis uses Monte Carlo simulation — the same methodology used by institutional investors, banks, and government agencies for risk-weighted pricing decisions. Each price point was tested across 5,000 simulated scenarios, with each scenario varying: market sentiment (±5%), interest rate impacts, lot size premiums, stage escalation factors, buyer demand signals, and price sensitivity. The model incorporates real comparable sales data, current market conditions, RBA rate forecasts, local demographic data, and historical sales velocity from previous stages at Woodchester Estate. The recommended price represents the optimal balance between maximising revenue and maintaining target sales velocity.
