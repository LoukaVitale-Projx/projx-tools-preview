Methodology

Monte Carlo Engine

5,000 simulations per lot using a monthly hazard-rate survival model. Each simulation perturbs the base monthly sale probability with Gaussian noise (σ=15%), plus a 35% chance of a rate-hike penalty (−8%). Cumulative probabilities computed at 3, 6, and 12 months.

Demand Drivers

Base probabilities calibrated to product type (Terrace Entry, Terrace Mid, Villa, Premium Villa). Adjusted for orientation (+12% NE, −15% West), shape (Corner +8%, Irregular −10%), and price sensitivity (−8% above $500k, −18% above $700k).

Market Adjustments

FHB incentive boost (+12%) applied to lots under $450k. Elevated vacancy headwind (−5% from 4.16%). Ripley Valley PDA growth tailwind (+7%). Competitor demand crowding from Providence (−3%). Combined net market modifier applied to all lots.

Buyer Profiling

Buyer mix (FHB/Investor/Upgrader/Downsizer) assigned by product category, reflecting price point, lot size, and market segment. Aggregate profile weighted by lot price. Useful for targeting marketing spend and incentive design.

Risk Flagging

Lots flagged as risk if 6-month sale probability falls below 45% OR price exceeds $600k. Risk reasons categorised as: High price point, Unfavourable attributes (south-facing, irregular), or Below-average absorption probability.

Limitations

Model does not account for marketing spend, incentive programs, staged release dynamics, or agent quality. Providence sell-out rate represents the ceiling for demand. Projections are probabilistic — actual results depend on execution, pricing decisions, and macro conditions at launch.
