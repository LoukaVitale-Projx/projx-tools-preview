Key Simulation Findings

Sell-out is release-schedule-constrained
All 10,000 scenarios converge on months 14–15. The release cadence (8 releases from Jul 2026 to Apr 2027) is the binding constraint, not demand. This means sell-out timing can be accelerated by compressing the release schedule — not by lowering prices.
Demand environment dominates (40.7% importance)
Base demand index is the #1 predictor. The $15–20k/month EOI campaign is the highest-leverage investment in the project. Every additional EOI registration directly reduces time-to-sell.
South Ripley premium is real (29.7% importance)
Being in South Ripley vs Ripley is the #2 predictor of faster sales. Providence overflow demand directly benefits Barrams Road. Providence selling out = Barrams Road's best marketing.
