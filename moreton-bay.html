Methodology & Criteria

What This Map Shows

Variable Definitions

📐
Site Area (ha)
Total land parcel size in hectares. Minimum 2ha required to be included in this analysis.
Min: 2.0 ha
🏗️
Developable Area (ha)
Net area after subtracting flood overlay, koala habitat, environmental buffers, waterways, and steep slope areas. This is the land you can actually build on.
Formula: Total − Flood − Koala − Env − Buffers
🌊
Flood %
Percentage of the site mapped as Medium or High Flood Risk under the MBRC Planning Scheme Flood Hazard Overlay. Sourced from the Moreton Bay interactive planning scheme.
Threshold: <10% preferred >30% = significant constraint
🐨
Koala Habitat %
Percentage of site covered by Core Koala Habitat under Queensland's DA Mapping system. Areas with >50% core habitat are generally not approvable for residential development.
Threshold: <10% preferred >50% = likely unapprovable
⛰️
Site Slope %
Average gradient across the developable area. Slope above 10% significantly increases civil construction costs (cut/fill, retaining walls, drainage) and can make standard lot subdivision unviable.
Pass: <10% Fail: >10% — NOT DEVELOPABLE
🔧
Sewer / Water Distance
Straight-line distance to the nearest reticulated sewer and water main. Sites beyond 2km typically require trunk main extensions funded by the developer — costs of $500K–$2M+ that kill feasibility on smaller sites.
Pass: <2km Fail: >2km — NOT NOW
🗺️
Zoning
Land use classification under the MBRC Planning Scheme. Emerging Community zones are the sweet spot — already identified for urban growth. Rural Residential requires rezoning. Rural is long-term only.

Rating Key

PRIME ✅
Score 13–15. Developable now. Emerging Community zoning, slope <10%, sewer <2km. Pursue immediately.
STRONG ✅
Score 10–12. Developable now but with one constraint (flood edge, koala fringe, or borderline services). Viable with mitigation.
WATCH ⚠️
Score 5–9. Not developable now. Sewer/water extension required or rezone needed. 3–7 year horizon. Monitor for infrastructure rollout.
FAIL ❌
Slope exceeds 10% and/or sewer >5km. Fundamental constraint that cannot be economically overcome on a standard residential project.
